## 🧑‍💻 Quick Summary

A major update to CrowdStrike, a leading endpoint security vendor, caused widespread Windows devices to **blue screen (BSOD)** and enter a **boot loop** 🌀 across companies, radio stations, and more, especially in Australia. Servers went offline, phone systems broke, and IT departments everywhere scrambled to fix critical failures.

## 🧑‍🔬 Technical Info & Context

- **CrowdStrike:** Popular cybersecurity software, notably Falcon, used for real-time endpoint protection 🦅.
- **Update:** A recent application update deployed via the cloud *mistakenly* triggered a catastrophic bug in Windows kernel-mode drivers, leading to BSODs 💣.
- **BSOD (Blue Screen of Death):** Windows error screen indicating a fatal system error—often hardware/driver related.
- **Boot loops:** Continuous failed restart attempts, rendering PCs unusable 🔄.
- **Scope:** Affected both users' laptops/desktops and critical back-end servers in multiple sectors. Some could recover after reboot, many could not without manual IT intervention.
